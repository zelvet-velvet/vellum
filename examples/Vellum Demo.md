# Vellum demo

A made-up budget app laid out in Vellum (icons by Lucide name; **Food** sits on This month's frame and keeps only its own title, summary and rows). The card below is the screen map; **Open editor** (or a tap on a screen) opens the editor: its own window on a computer, the full screen on a phone.

> [!tip]- Using it
> - **Top bar**: ✕ closes (Esc), the screen's name opens the list of screens (+ beside a screen adds one opened from it, the device at the foot), undo / redo, Save.
> - **The phone** sits in the middle. Click a block to choose it, click again on a text or shape inside to choose that; drag to move (Alt: 1 px steps), the block's corner to resize; double-click a text to type in it. A click beside the phone lets go.
> - **The pill** at the foot: Edit (frames and safe areas) / Show (the page alone), zoom (Ctrl+wheel on a computer), Fit.
> - **The panel** (right on a computer, a sheet on the phone) shows only what is chosen; with nothing chosen on a computer, the screen's own settings.
> - Keys: arrows nudge 1 px (Shift 4), Delete, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+D duplicate, Ctrl+S save, Esc goes up a level. Unsaved work stays on the device until saved.
